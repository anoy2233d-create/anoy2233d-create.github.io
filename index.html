01 — About

Engineering efficiency on the shop floor

I'm an Industrial & Production Engineering graduate from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), working at the intersection of operations, quality and data.

Today, as Officer, Operation Excellence at Akij Light Engineering Ltd., I run time studies to set Standard Minute Values, balance lines, coordinate raw-material flow with Supply Chain, and drive continuous-improvement projects on the floor.

My toolkit blends classical industrial engineering — Value Stream Mapping, Time & Motion Study, Root Cause Analysis, Lean, Six Sigma, TQM and QC tools — with modern analytics (Excel, Power BI, Python and SQL) so decisions are evidence-based, not guesses.

I've also published research on e-commerce logistics and actively lead volunteer teams — I like solving problems and building people at the same time.
