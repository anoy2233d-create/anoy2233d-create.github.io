01 — About

I find the minutes a factory gives away.

I'm an Industrial & Production Engineering graduate from RUET, working where operations, quality and data meet. Most of what I do comes down to one question: where is this process losing time, and what will it take to get it back?

As Officer, Operation Excellence at Akij Light Engineering Ltd., I run time studies to set SMVs, rebalance lines so no station becomes the bottleneck, coordinate raw-material flow with Supply Chain, and run continuous-improvement projects on the floor.

My toolkit blends classical industrial engineering — VSM, time & motion study, PMTS, root cause analysis, Lean, Six Sigma and DMAIC — with Excel, Power BI, Python and SQL, so a recommendation arrives with the numbers already attached.

  • Implemented Short Interval Control (SIC) — disciplined 5-minute stand-up meetings every 2 hours to catch and resolve production issues the same day instead of losing a full day's output.
